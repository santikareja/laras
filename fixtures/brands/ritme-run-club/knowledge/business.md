# Ritme Run Club

> Fictional brand for Laras test fixtures. All names, places, and prices are made up.

## Summary
A free community running club in Jakarta with weekly group runs and two paid race events per year. Started by five friends in 2023, now around 1,200 members.

## Programs and prices
| Program | Price |
|---|---|
| Sunday group run (5K / 10K pace groups) | Free |
| Wednesday night interval session | Free |
| Ritme 10K City Race (registration) | IDR 250,000 |
| Ritme Half Marathon (registration) | IDR 450,000 |
| Club singlet | IDR 180,000 |

## Audience
- Office workers aged 20–40 who want a social reason to run.
- Beginners who feel intimidated by competitive running groups.

## What makes us different
- Pace groups from 5:00 to 8:30 min/km, so nobody runs alone.
- Every run ends with coffee and stretching, not with a leaderboard.
- Race entry fees fund free water stations on Sunday runs.

## Competitors
Performance-focused clubs that publish leaderboards and target experienced runners.

## FAQ
- **Do I need to register for Sunday runs?** No. Just show up at 5:45 AM at the meeting point.
- **I have never run 5K. Can I join?** Yes. The 8:30 pace group walks and runs.
- **Where do race fees go?** Event costs first, the rest funds Sunday water stations.
